About Us

Bringing the Taste of Brazil to the Heart of Dublin

Founded in 2019, Fabi's Grill Parnell was created with a simple mission: to share the authentic flavors of Brazilian cuisine with the people of Dublin. Since opening its doors, the restaurant has become a welcoming destination for both the Brazilian community and food lovers eager to experience the rich culinary traditions of Brazil.

Located in the city center, Fabi's Grill combines traditional recipes, quality ingredients, and warm hospitality to create a genuine Brazilian dining experience. Every dish is prepared with care, celebrating the flavors, culture, and spirit that make Brazilian food so special.

Over the years, Fabi's Grill has earned a reputation for generous portions, authentic recipes, and a commitment to customer satisfaction. Signature dishes such as Picanha, Feijoada, Parmegiana, Stroganoff, and a variety of traditional Brazilian snacks have made the restaurant a favorite among locals and visitors alike.

More than just a restaurant, Fabi's Grill is a place where people come together to enjoy great food, celebrate Brazilian culture, and create memorable moments with family and friends.

🎯 Our Mission

To deliver the authentic taste of Brazil through high-quality food, exceptional service, and a welcoming atmosphere that makes every guest feel at home.

👁️ Our Vision

To be recognized as Ireland's leading Brazilian restaurant, connecting people through outstanding food, culture, and hospitality.
